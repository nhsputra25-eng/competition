We Started With
One Street.

There was a pile of plastic waste on our street that never seemed to end. Every week it was cleaned up, and every week it came back. We got tired of just complaining—so we started taking notes.

What We Noticed

For the first three months, we sat at the neighborhood watch post every Saturday morning. We counted plastic bottles, tracked when people threw away their trash, and asked our neighbors why they didn't sort it. The answer wasn't laziness—it was that there was no convenient place to sort waste, and there wasn't enough reason for people to take the extra effort.

From those notes, we realized the problem wasn't people—it was the system. That was the starting point for everything on this site.

Who We Are

We're a small team of three: one who likes working with data, one who likes making things, and one who enjoys talking to people. We're around 19 years old. We're not a startup or a big community—we're just people who live on the same street.

What We've Done So Far

  • 2023 – First field notes, with 12 households tracked.
  • 2024 – First version of a digital waste bank tested in one neighborhood block.
  • 2025 – Two nearby blocks asked to try the same system.
  • 2026 – A small composting idea for boarding houses was tested in two rental homes.
